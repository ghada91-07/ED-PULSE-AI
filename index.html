ℹ️ About & Methodology

ED PULSE AI is a prototype designed to support emergency department operational awareness, patient-flow monitoring and resource planning.

The current prototype uses synthetic scenarios and transparent rule-based calculations. It is not a trained machine-learning model, a validated clinical risk score, or a medical decision-making system.

Future development may include predictive models trained and evaluated using appropriately authorized, de-identified operational data.

Demonstration only. No real patient data. Clinical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.
