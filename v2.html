ℹ️ About & Methodology

ED PULSE AI is a prototype for monitoring emergency department operations, patient flow, consultation delays, and nursing workload.

This version uses synthetic data and rule-based calculations. The 60-minute outlook is illustrative, not a machine-learning prediction.

Future development may include a trained and independently evaluated forecasting model using authorized, de-identified operational data.

Demonstration only. Not validated for clinical decisions or real hospital deployment.
