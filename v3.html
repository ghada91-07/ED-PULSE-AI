ℹ️ About & Methodology

ED PULSE AI is a proof-of-concept dashboard for emergency department patient-flow monitoring and operational decision support.

All patient counts, delays, recommendations and projections are synthetic. The current prototype uses transparent, rule-based calculations. It does not process real patient information, provide clinical decisions or claim validated AI performance.

Future development may include a machine-learning model trained and evaluated on appropriately authorized, de-identified operational data.
